# Passages — original P6 practice texts

British school English. Line numbers follow the layout below and are the references used in the questions (for example, “line 3”). Decorative photos may be added later when the pages are typeset; **no question requires a picture**.

Heading-matching scheme (Set 1): the **Introduction** heading is already placed. The pupil matches the other five headings from the bank. Online, this is **one scored item** (`S1-H01`): all five boxes must be correct for the item to score. See `online-spec.md`.

**Exam stem**

H. Maya is reading a webpage about jobs in the community. Read it.

**Webpage title**

Jobs in the Community: Leisure Centre Lifeguard

**Heading bank** (printed with the questions; all six labels appear, even though Introduction is already on the page)

| Entry requirements | Skills & Qualities | Introduction |

**Layout notes**

- Framed as a simple webpage / browser window, like the source paper.
- Left margin: line numbers. Right margin: optional decorative photo of a pool or a lifeguard (not needed to answer).

**Page title**

Harbour Lights Job Fair  
Find a job that suits you!

**Layout notes**

- 2×2 grid of four short advertisements, then a contact footer, like the source paper.
- Line numbers run in this order: title, top-left ad, top-right ad, bottom-left ad, bottom-right ad, footer. (When typeset in two columns, keep these line numbers unchanged so any later line reference still works.)
- Optional small pin / card artwork only; every question is answerable from the text.
- The four jobs are not the source jobs (writer, photographer, vet’s assistant, cook’s assistant).

**Dictionary box for Set 2 Question 3** (printed with the questions, not on the advertisement page)

The target word is **train** in the Green Wheel Tours advertisement (line 17: “We will train you in the first two weeks.”).

```
train n
1  a railway engine and carriages that carry people or goods
   'We took the early train to Sha Tin.'
2  a long line of people, animals or things moving together
   'A train of camels crossed the desert.'

train v
3  to teach a person or animal the skills for a job or activity
   'The club will train new members every summer.'
4  to prepare for a sports event by practising
   'She trains at the pool for two hours a day.'
```
